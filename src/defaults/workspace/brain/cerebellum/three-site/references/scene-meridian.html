drag · tap · scroll

01 — Surface

A sheet of light that behaves like cloth. Drag it and it follows; tap it and it rings; leave it and it breathes.

02 — Pressure

Scroll, and the same surface hardens. The folds sharpen into facets, the warmth drains into glass.

03 — Memory

Everything you touched is still in the surface, faint, until it forgets. Go back up to soften it again.
