01 — How it works

Three shells, one proof

COREThe ledger crystal

A compact state root. Every balance in the network is a facet; nothing leaves the core without a signature.

LATTICEThe consensus cage

Validators hold the lattice from the outside. A block is final when the cage closes around it.

SATELLITESRelays in orbit

Light clients that carry proofs to phones and browsers. They never touch the core.

02 — Network
